# My Favorite Movie: Avatar: The Way of Water 🌊

![Avatar: The Way of Water](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/8/89/Avatar_The_Way_of_Water_poster.jpg)

## 🎬 Overview
**Avatar: The Way of Water** is a 2022 epic science fiction film directed by **James Cameron**.  
It is the sequel to the 2009 blockbuster *Avatar* and continues the story of **Jake Sully** and **Neytiri** as they protect their family and the planet **Pandora** from human invaders.

## 💡 Why I Like It
- The visuals and underwater effects are absolutely stunning.  
- It delivers a strong environmental message about protecting nature.  
- The theme of family and unity is deeply emotional.  

## 🌱 Main Characters
1. Jake Sully (Sam Worthington)  
2. Neytiri (Zoe Saldaña)  
3. Kiri (Sigourney Weaver)  
4. Lo'ak (Britain Dalton)  
5. Tonowari (Cliff Curtis)
